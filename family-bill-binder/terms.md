## What the app is

Binder is a manual household bill record and family handoff tool. It helps you write down bill details, local reminder timing, paid status, owner labels, notes, recurring history, and a binder PDF for people you choose to share with. Optional iCloud sync lets invited household participants edit a shared binder.

## What the app is not

- It does not pay bills, move money, verify balances, link bank accounts, aggregate accounts, provide credit scores, or make financial decisions.

- It is not financial, legal, tax, credit, accounting, or estate-planning advice.

- You are responsible for checking billers, account portals, mailed statements, due dates, payment confirmation, and any family instructions before acting.

## Your content

The bill information, notes, PDFs, and exports you create belong to you. We do not claim ownership of your content. Your content stays on your device unless you enable iCloud, join a shared household, or export or share it through iOS. Household members with edit access can change the shared records. The activity feed records actions taken in Binder, not verified bank transfers. Offline edits sync when iCloud becomes available; background updates are not guaranteed to arrive immediately. iCloud accounts, sufficient storage, and Apple service availability are required.

## Free and Pro access

- Free access includes five active bills, two lists, and two notes per household (with unlimited list items), private iCloud sharing, activity history, calendars, reminders, night theme, recaps, and PDF and data exports.

- The organizer’s active Pro subscription unlocks unlimited active bills, lists, and notes for their household. Participants do not each need Pro. Existing bills and history stay usable if the subscription ends; the limit applies to new additions and reactivating bills. Household entitlement updates require periodic connectivity.

- We may adjust features or limits for future versions, but existing local records remain yours.
